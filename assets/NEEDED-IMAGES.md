# Photographs still needed

The site is built and live-ready, but no product photography was supplied. Every
image slot currently shows a branded placeholder sized to the real dimensions, so
adding a photograph is an upload in the admin — no layout work, nothing to
rebuild by hand.

**How to add one:** go to `kaybeint.com/keystatic`, open the product, use the
**Photographs** field, upload, add alt text, save.

---

## Priority 1 — one photograph per product

These replace the placeholders that a visitor sees first. One good shot each is
enough to transform the site.

| Product | Slot aspect | Minimum size | What the shot should show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plummer Blocks | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A complete housing, ideally an SN-series block, three-quarter angle so the split line and bolt holes read |
| Adapter Sleeves | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A sleeve on its own, threaded end visible; a group of two or three sizes together works well |
| Withdrawal Sleeves | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A sleeve showing the tapered body and threaded end |
| Locating Rings | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | One or two rings, flat on, or fitted in a housing |
| Lock Nuts | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A nut showing the slots clearly, angled |
| Lock Washers | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A washer showing the inner and outer tabs |
| Bearings | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 px | A thrust bearing, or a small group of stocked bearings |

## Priority 2 — homepage hero

| Slot | Aspect | Minimum size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | 5:4 | 1400 × 1120 px | The strongest single image available. A grouped arrangement of housings and sleeves, or a clean shot of stock on the shelf. Shot on a plain light background works best against the blue hero panel. |

## Priority 3 — nice to have

- **Additional angles per product.** Each product supports a gallery, so second
  and third images stack on the product page automatically.
- **Premises or stock photograph** for the About page — conveys "ready stock" far
  better than words.
- **Brand logos** for MASTA, AEC, USA, MTN, VARUNA, AND, GEM, SKILL and PB.
  Vector (SVG, AI, EPS, CDR) or PNG at 400 px wide minimum with a transparent
  background. Until supplied, every brand shows as a typographic card, which is
  deliberate — consistent type reads better than two logos beside seven gaps.

## Shooting notes

Nothing elaborate is required. A phone camera in good light is genuinely fine.

- **Plain background.** A sheet of white or mid-grey paper or card. Avoid busy
  workshop backgrounds.
- **Diffuse light.** Near a window or in shade, not direct sun, and not on-camera
  flash. Hard flash on machined steel produces blown-out hotspots.
- **Fill the frame.** Get close. The part should occupy most of the image.
- **Landscape orientation**, since every slot is wider than it is tall.
- **Show scale where useful.** For sleeves, several sizes together communicates
  range better than one part alone.
- **Clean parts.** Wipe off oil and swarf; it shows more than you would expect.

## What not to do

- Don't use manufacturers' catalogue images or photographs taken from other
  websites. They are copyrighted, and using them creates a real legal exposure.
  Photographs of your own stock are both safer and more persuasive.
- Don't upload images straight from a DSLR without resizing. Anything above about
  2000 px wide is wasted bandwidth for a visitor on mobile data.
